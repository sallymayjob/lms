Executive Summary

A governed production
system for learning

The Slack LMS Content Factory is a multi-agent AI pipeline that designs, validates, quality-assures, and delivers practitioner-grade micro-learning content natively inside Slack. In place of a traditional LMS requiring expensive software licences, complex onboarding, and dedicated content teams, the system produces 288 lessons across a 12-month employee pathway — at a total build cost of approximately $1,400.

Core Design Principle
The system does not replace human judgement. It eliminates the administrative and production overhead that typically consumes instructional designers, freeing the team to focus on curation, strategy, and mentorship.

Rather than procuring an LMS or commissioning an external agency, the team built an internal AI-powered content factory — lessons delivered natively in Slack, the tool teams already use daily.

Quality Framework

SOP-05 — Six
weighted dimensions

Every lesson passes through the SOP-05 scoring system before it reaches a learner. No lesson clears the pipeline without meeting a minimum standard across all six dimensions.

20%
ULC Completeness
All 7 sections present in correct order: Hook, Core Content, Insight, Takeaway, Mission, Verification, Submit Block.
20%
Content Accuracy
Practitioner-level knowledge. No hallucinated claims. No generic advice that could apply to any profession.
20%
Mission Feasibility
Verb-first, completable under 5 minutes, uses tools the learner already has, includes an alternative path.
15%
Word Limits
Total ≤ 500 words. Core content ≤ 300. Insight ≤ 50. Takeaway ≤ 15 — every limit enforced at output.
15%
Brand Compliance
Brand score ≥ 80. Zero banned terminology in final output. Emoji count ≤ 2 from approved list only.
10%
Continuity
No repetition from the previous 5 lessons. Each lesson advances the learner, not restate prior content.

Key Learnings

What we discovered
building at scale

Relevant to any organisation considering AI-driven content production. These are the patterns that emerged — not from theory, but from running 200+ lessons through the system.

Content Integrity is Paramount
Systematic audits revealed that field misalignment in the content database created silent delivery failures — authored content diverged from what was delivered in Slack without any visible error. Solution: always check both property fields and page body content during updates. They are not automatically synchronised.
Fabricated Statistics Are a Systemic Risk
Unverified quantitative claims — "3x more likely", "23% more" — appeared consistently in AI-generated insight sections without research backing. All quantitative claims without explicit sourcing are now replaced with directional language. This is a hard rule enforced at the content generation stage, not caught at QA.
Human Oversight Remains Non-Negotiable
The system eliminates production overhead — not human judgement. The 70% rule applies: the majority of resources should be directed toward curation, review, and quality assurance. Expect 144–432 hours of human oversight for production-ready content across 288 lessons. Plan for it rather than hoping to avoid it.
Batch Processing Outperforms Sequential Generation
Processing 20+ lessons simultaneously through the pipeline maintains consistency and reduces overhead far more effectively than sequential individual generation. The system is optimised for batch operations by design — treat it as a production line, not a one-at-a-time tool.
